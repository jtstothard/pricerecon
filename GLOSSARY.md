# PriceRecon buying decisions

Language for monitoring specific products and discovering acceptable buying options.

## Language

**Buyer-approved alternative set**:
A named group of distinct product configurations explicitly accepted by the buyer as buying options, subject to their requirements and exclusions. Membership expresses buying acceptability, not identical goods or proven compatibility with a build.
_Avoid_: Identical products, interchangeable products


**Build compatibility**:
The suitability of a particular product configuration for a specified PC build, supported by evidence for the relevant requirements. Exact product identity and buyer-approved alternative membership do not establish build compatibility.
_Avoid_: Fits (without build context and evidence)
