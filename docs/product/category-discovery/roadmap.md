# Staged roadmap and decision gates

These stages order learning and release scope; they are not estimates or authorised builds. All stages share [one ticket graph](tickets/README.md).

The buyer accepted the [watch-first release boundary and later-phase deferrals](tickets/release-route.md) on 2026-10-27. Planning decisions are complete; live proof and operational gates remain open. The PC design challenge has been completed as research; PC workflow proof follows watch workflow proof. Fountain pens and personal-benefit execution remain later phases.

1. **Foundation decisions:** qualify supported source routes and access constraints; exact-configuration evidence; observation persistence; UK cost and discount scenario boundaries. Exit: source receipts/protocol, model proposal and verification plans make false bargains detectable. Existing monitoring remains a compatibility requirement. Resolve the missing-history-writer question before promising historical pricing.
2. **Watch proof:** gather an agreed cohort, compare candidate baseline methods and sampling strategies, and evaluate the [full watch workflow](vertical-slice.md), including no-coupon and unresolved evidence. Exit: buyer-reviewed explanation, defensible or withheld baselines, qualified costs and a reproducible public-coupon case where available. Blocked evidence remains a visible gate, not a reason to fabricate a successful slice.
3. **First useful release decision:** watch discovery feed with editable preferences, explainable comparisons, correction flow, tracking/alerts and coverage/health visibility. Release only after the acceptance and operating-budget tickets resolve and separate implementation authority exists. Broad discovery is over supported sources, including disclosed failed/capped coverage. Do not require every product to have enough history before it can appear as an uncertain candidate.
4. **Generalisation:** challenge shared contracts with PC parts before scaling category-specific rules. Preserve all intended PC identity dimensions, conditions and components; use results to revise the shared boundary. Fountain pens follow as an intended category, with nib/material/filling/finish and replacement-part distinctions.
5. **Personal benefits:** after public coupons and shared compatibility semantics, investigate Perks at Work, UNiDAYS and Blue Light; TopCashback/Quidco; discounted gift cards and account credit. Account access and execution require a later mandate. Show personal eligibility, public totals, compatible alternative payment scenarios and uncertain cashback separately. No speculative integrations now.

Before estimating, learn merchant access/quotas and permitted retention, usable historical coverage, observation cadence and elapsed collection needs, browser/AI cost per useful offer, failure/recheck rate, correction burden and operator maintenance time. Quote ranges with assumptions only after those measurements; no calendar or staffing precision is justified by this source audit.
