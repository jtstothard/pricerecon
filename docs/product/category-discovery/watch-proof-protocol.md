# Watch workflow acceptance protocol

Accepted by the buyer as the planning specification, 2026-10-27. Owned by [What evidence would accept the complete watch workflow?](tickets/watch-proof.md). This specification defines future proof; no test or live pass is reported here.

## Two acceptance stages

**Protocol accepted** means the buyer accepts the cases, expected outcomes and evidence gates below. It closes the planning decision and permits the PC-parts design challenge. **Workflow proven** requires implementation, deterministic verification and separately authorised live evidence to pass the applicable gates. Neither prototype approval nor protocol acceptance establishes workflow proof or authorises acquisition, implementation or activation.

Preserve specific-item monitoring while adding preference-based and category discovery for one UK buyer. ## Alert and capacity policy agreed by the buyer

Send one alert when a verified UK delivered total crosses its target. Suppress repeats for the same offer/price. Alert again after a further price drop, or after rising above target and later crossing back below. Corrections, stale evidence and source recovery alone do not trigger bargain alerts. Uncertain discoveries remain visible in the feed; unknown or invalidated totals cannot trigger verified threshold alerts.

Existing tracking and verification of potential alerts take priority over discovery breadth. Reduce discovery first under a cap, disclose coverage gaps and withhold alerts whose required evidence cannot be refreshed. Numerical cadence/freshness and any additional notification frequency settings remain execution prerequisites, not invented defaults. Later tests must exercise the alert policy across restart, duplicate leads and multiple acquisition routes; unresolved identity cannot certify a new qualifying offer.

## Evidence receipts and result reporting

Deterministic cases record fixture provenance, build/policy versions, input, expected and observed outcome, assertions and failures. Include boundary, negative and recovery cases; a mock pass demonstrates logic only.

Live receipts record timestamp, source/access lane, exact selection, seller/condition/purchase mode where applicable, UK destination scenario, availability, price components, coupon state, coverage/caps, evidence pointer, versions and limitations/recheck needs. Retention and access must permit the evidence. Checkout verification stops before purchase; login, basket or account actions require their own authorised scope. Real retained history cannot be replaced by fixtures.

Report each gate as passed, failed, blocked or unproven with its evidence pointer and claim scope. A blocked source is distinct from no offers; a passed no-coupon path is distinct from coupon-success proof. Preserve gaps in the overall verdict. No full monitoring, complete coverage or general best-deal claim follows from a subset of passing cases.

## Mandatory gate before live execution or recurring activation

Approve numerical provider/request/page/detail/retry limits, browser concurrency/time, AI calls/tokens/spend where applicable, storage/log limits, study duration, spend and operator-time ceilings. Agree per-source retention/deletion/backup treatment, freshness schedule and a named maintenance owner with stop/resume responsibilities. Validate source entitlements, intended-use/retention rights and actual shared cap enforcement. Reuse native infrastructure under the operating decision.

These settings remain unsettled here. No live study, new collection, account connection or production polling is authorised. Future operational scope must be concrete and separately approved; this protocol can be accepted while execution remains gated.
