# Bounded first watch workflow

This is a future verification protocol and discussion asset, not a claim that the workflow has been built or verified. Start with a small named watch cohort and declared source roster agreed in [source qualification](tickets/source-qualification.md). Include an AliExpress route, eBay/second-hand, a brand-direct route, UK retail, a UK-shipping international route, and search/aggregator/community leads. If a required route is blocked, disclose it and keep the full-coverage gate open; do not replace AliExpress silently.

## Candidate evidence corpus

The user supplied a prior 94-watch study. [Corpus, conflicts and proposed cohort](watch-research-context.md) identify concrete ML05/ML05B, AD2066, SN0144 and Merkur Lightning cases, plus revision, fit and coupon examples. These are dated research inputs, not current qualified offers or established price history. Select the final cohort through source qualification.

## End-to-end path

Discover watch candidates independently of existing tracked-item rules → retain discovery origins → resolve exact configurations or withhold identity → collect comparable available merchant offers → persist repeated evidence and gaps → establish or honestly withhold usual price → validate a public coupon if one is available → compute the UK cost scenario → show an illustrated explanation with evidence and coverage → let a buyer correct, refine preferences or track the item.

No actual watch references, prices or coupons below are presented as observed offers. Select real examples only during authorised evidence collection. Images must have provenance and an access/retention policy.

| Representative case | Required evidence / expected result |
| --- | --- |
| Qualified recurring-price watch with public coupon | Repeated available same-reference/size/dial/strap offers; dated ordinary sales/codes; exact eligible basket/destination and coupon terms. Show usual-price basis, current best comparable total, dated observed low and reproduced coupon total; reject incompatible stacks. Coupon success cannot compensate for missing history. |
| Complete no-coupon watch | Same identity/history/cost gates; record “no verified applicable public coupon found in checked routes,” not universal absence. Deal explanation and delivered-price tracking remain useful without a coupon. |
| Newly discovered colourful watch | Limited observations or unavailable shipping; display potential deal, limited evidence, unknown baseline and unresolved total. No percentage saving, definitive best offer or below-£60-delivered alert. Product can remain discoverable in an explicitly uncertain view. |
| Multi-variant AliExpress listing | Similar reference, different movement/size/dial/bracelet; cheapest displayed SKU is accessory or undesired variant. Preserve SKU selection and evidence; parent PID/first SKU/title match cannot certify target variant. |
| Used eBay watch and auction | Condition description/photos and purchase mode, postage and seller. Separate used cohort; current bid excluded from buy-now comparison. Flag visible damage and unknown authenticity/internal health; do not invent an authenticity verdict. |
| International GBP listing | Prove dispatch, UK destination and tax inclusion. Test VAT already collected, additional import exposure, threshold/basket changes, FX/shipping/carrier unknowns. Prevent double VAT; unresolved liability means conditional/unknown total. Exact legal rules require current authoritative research. |
| Coupon traps | Minimum spend/quantity, expiry/time zone, membership, app/new-customer, coins/redemption limits and payment offer. Preserve ordinary public scenario alongside conditional/unverified opportunities; do not assume a logged-in/new-account price is universal. |
| Stale or conflicting evidence | Search snippet is cheaper than merchant, offer out of stock, seller changes variant or currency, source fails, duplicate affiliate lead appears. Recheck or withhold ranking/alert; preserve lineage and explain why evidence was excluded. |
| Preference and correction | “Compact, colourful, under £150” interpreted as editable constraints; unknown size stays unknown. Correct an inferred identity and observe dependent assessment/alert invalidation; retain correction history. |

## Release gate

Every qualifying card shows image/source, exact configuration/condition, merchant and purchase mode; timestamp/destination; item/shipping/tax/other components; coupon state and terms; usual/current-best/observed-low/personal-price labels with unavailable states; evidence links and exclusion reasons; checked coverage and source health. Buyer can distinguish the four price/benefit states without reading operator documentation.

Require deterministic fixture evidence for the difficult cases and bounded live read-only merchant verification receipts for each claimed source route. A receipt records timestamp, access lane, selection, destination, availability, evidence pointer, limitations and expiry/recheck needs. Checkout reproduction must stop before purchase and must be separately scoped if it needs login or basket interaction. Current task authorises none of that execution. Retained real history cannot be replaced by synthetic observations. If no reproducible public coupon exists, the no-coupon workflow may qualify, but the coupon-success case stays unproven.

A later PC-parts challenge must repeat the path with a near-name model mismatch (for example base/Ti), capacity/configuration mismatch, used versus new and component-only listing. Pass only if shared evidence/cost/pricing contracts survive without category forks; otherwise revise before broader rollout.
