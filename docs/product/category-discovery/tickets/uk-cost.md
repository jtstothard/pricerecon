## Question

How should exact-offer UK cost scenarios account for shipping, FX, VAT/import treatment, possible duty and carrier fees without presenting uncertain liabilities as verified totals?

## Evidence required

Research current authoritative UK guidance and source-specific tax collection evidence, with dated primary sources. Compare destination, dispatch origin, basket/consignment thresholds, quantities, tax-inclusive marketplace checkout, shipping and payment FX contexts. Examine missing currency/price defaults and item-price-only filters. Record exact variant and destination; do not assume GBP implies UK dispatch.

Starting context: [source audit](../audit.md), [domain proposal](../domain.md), [product brief](../brief.md), [slice](../vertical-slice.md). These are inputs, not accepted answers.

Additional input: [prior watch corpus, manifest and proposed cases](../watch-research-context.md). Use the source conflicts as investigation cases; do not accept the prior evidence labels or prices without reconciliation.

## Decision and acceptance gate

Present a cost-component/status contract and worked evidence-backed examples covering VAT already paid, imports, thresholds, missing shipping/fees and FX. Verified versus conditional versus unknown totals must follow evidence. Avoid double VAT and zero defaults. Legal values/rules remain subject to current verification; do not implement a speculative tax engine.
