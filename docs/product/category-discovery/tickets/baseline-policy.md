## Question

Which evidence and policy should distinguish usual obtainable price, current best comparable offer, observed dated historical low and personal eligible price?

## Evidence required

Compare candidate approaches against real retained observations where available; otherwise state data gaps and propose collection before selection. Evaluate time windows and cadence, routine sales/codes, promotion duration, seller/time diversity, outliers, minimum evidence, sparse/new history, market shifts, stale/out-of-stock offers, variant changes and new/used cohorts. Consider sensitivity and bias from merchant duplication, search ranking and eligibility. Do not pick a formula by convention.

Starting context: [source audit](../audit.md), [domain proposal](../domain.md), [product brief](../brief.md), [slice](../vertical-slice.md). These are inputs, not accepted answers.

## Decision and acceptance gate

Document alternatives and trade-offs, required empirical evidence and transparent explanations. Choose or defer window/cadence/sufficiency and change detection with reasons; do not invent thresholds without evidence. Insufficient history yields potential deal/limited evidence with no fabricated percentage. “Current best” and “historical low” carry coverage, date and eligibility, never global certainty.
