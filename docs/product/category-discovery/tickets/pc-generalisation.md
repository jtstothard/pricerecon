## Resolution comments

### 2026-10-27 — Resolution: reuse with explicit category and shared-contract obligations

Accept [the PC-parts design challenge](../pc-generalisation-research.md) as the research answer. Its primary-source counterexamples cover GPU generation/capacity/board variants, RAM kits, SSD configuration, complete systems, boxed/tray processors and merchant condition grades. They support reuse of the shared acquisition/evidence/cost/baseline machinery as a planning proposal, with PC policy and four explicit shared obligations: composition distinct from quantity; exact identity distinct from approved alternatives and compatibility; offer commitments distinct from catalogue definitions; and provenance-backed market-regime/continuity review. Do not flatten kits, pool unlike cohorts or infer component prices from bundles. No separate PC store, scheduler, costing or baseline architecture is justified by these cases; later evidence requiring such forks must reopen the boundary.

The asset reconciles dated Scan direct/solver receipts with fresh readable documentation, without treating either as current connector, selected-offer, permission or durable-history qualification. It drafts the complete PC workflow and deterministic/live proof gates using the watch protocol's two stages and passed/failed/blocked/unproven outcomes. This meets the research gate, not PC protocol acceptance, workflow proof, current price/history validation or operational approval.

Two sharp buyer choices are now separate children: [Should PC discovery include buyer-approved alternatives?](pc-alternatives.md) and [What PC compatibility promise should the product make?](pc-compatibility.md). Both precede the release-route decision. Numerical operational choices and source rights remain existing pre-execution gates. Fountain-pen identity/composition requirements and later primary-source validation remain preserved as the next category lens; taxonomy stays in the map's fog. No implementation, collection, account linking, deployment, commit or push was performed.
