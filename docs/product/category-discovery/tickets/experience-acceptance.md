## Question

How should an illustrated feed, preference interpretation, evidence detail, corrections and alerts communicate trustworthy and uncertain deals for one UK buyer?

## Evidence required

Use prototype and domain-modeling to prepare a disposable discussion asset after prerequisites. Include all vertical-slice cases, exact £60-delivered tracking, compact/colourful under-£150 discovery and products not already tracked. Show distinct public/personal/cashback/payment scenarios, evidence age, checked coverage, source health and missing data. Plan image provenance. Ask the buyer to interpret cards and correct a wrong identity.

Starting context: [source audit](../audit.md), [domain proposal](../domain.md), [product brief](../brief.md), [slice](../vertical-slice.md). These are inputs, not accepted answers.

## Decision and acceptance gate

Only the user accepts usability/noise/condition preferences. Record live feedback, unresolved misunderstandings and the correction/alert policy. A prototype is not product implementation or live source proof. Keep overly broad personal claims and unknown-cost alerts from appearing as verified bargains.

### 2026-10-27 — Frontend design critique requested

[Design review and proposed revision direction](../experience-design-review.md) identifies missing feed-level evaluation, weak price hierarchy, contradictory old claims after correction/staleness, conflated alert qualification/threshold states and an insufficient correction interaction. ### 2026-10-27 — Image-led comparison feed direction approved

The buyer approved an **image-led comparison feed**. Revised [the disposable prototype](../experience-prototype.html) with eight simultaneous illustrated product rows, prominent public delivered totals, expandable evidence, editable tracking targets, preference controls and field/value identity corrections. Reviewer controls are separated from the buyer feed. Illustrations remain original fictional SVGs, not source-qualified product photos.

Corrected/stale assessments are withheld and their prior claims explicitly labelled historical. Alert states distinguish an above-target verified price from missing verification, changed identity and preference mismatch. The £64 public-price fixture now waits for a price fall at a £60 target rather than implying insufficient evidence.

728px and 375px previews rendered without reported console errors. In-browser scripted smoke checks covered below/above target, correction invalidation, stale evidence and unknown total; no assertion failures were reported. These checks do not establish buyer usability or full keyboard/accessibility acceptance. Approval establishes the feed direction only; uncertainty placement, condition preferences, alert frequency/noise and live task interpretation remain pending. Ticket stays open.


### 2026-10-27 — Resolution: revised experience approved by buyer

After reviewing the revised image-led comparison feed, the buyer replied **“approve”**. Accept the disposable prototype as the planning direction: simultaneous illustrated candidates, prominent public UK delivered price, expandable price/evidence detail, editable preferences and price targets, and field/value corrections that preserve original evidence while withholding dependent assessments. Uncertain candidates remain visible with explicit missing-data labels; used buy-now candidates remain distinguishable and can be excluded through preferences. These are editable prototype defaults, not inferred permanent personal preferences.

Accept the correction/alert explanation policy: retain tracking intent; distinguish below-target, above-target, outside-preference, stale/unverified and identity-disputed states; withhold verified threshold alerts for unknown delivered totals or withdrawn assessments. Keep personal, public, payment and cashback scenarios separate. The asset and prior comments retain fixture coverage, image provenance and preview/smoke-check evidence.

Approval is direct buyer acceptance of the presented planning prototype. No task-by-task buyer interpretation transcript, measured noise tolerance, live-source proof, accessibility audit or production acceptance is claimed. Notification frequency/deduplication and numerical freshness remain for the operating-envelope and complete-watch-workflow decisions. No implementation, deployment, commit or push is authorised by this resolution.
