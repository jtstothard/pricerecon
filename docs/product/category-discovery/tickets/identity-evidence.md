## Question

How should source product/listing/SKU identities, category configurations, claim provenance and corrections compose without treating title matches as exactness?

## Evidence required

Build an evidence corpus of watch references/generations/sizes/movements/dials/straps and accessories; trace AliExpress first-SKU/title-confidence behaviour, eBay purchase-mode/condition fields, Shopify store identity and variant links. Contrast manufacturer specs, seller claims, extracted text/images, AI inference and human correction. Challenge with PC capacity/model/component cases and pen/nib requirements. Research identifier authority and field conflicts.

Starting context: [source audit](../audit.md), [domain proposal](../domain.md), [product brief](../brief.md), [slice](../vertical-slice.md). These are inputs, not accepted answers.

Additional input: [prior watch corpus, manifest and proposed cases](../watch-research-context.md). Use the source conflicts as investigation cases; do not accept the prior evidence labels or prices without reconciliation.

## Decision and acceptance gate

Produce a proposed identity/provenance contract, uncertain/conflicting states, correction/invalidation policy and category/shared boundary. Demonstrate that title-token equality, product PID and repeated AI claims cannot certify a variant; photos cannot prove authenticity or internal health. Keep this proposal separate from schema implementation.

Accept the [identity evidence research and counterexample corpus](../identity-evidence-research.md) as the contract for downstream investigation. Corrections append lineage and invalidate affected bindings, cohorts, assessments and alerts rather than transferring a wrong variant's price to the intended one.
