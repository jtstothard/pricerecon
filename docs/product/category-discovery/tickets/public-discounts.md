## Question

How are coupons found, scoped, validated and combined for exact offers while preserving no-coupon and unverified states?

## Evidence required

Investigate merchant/platform public coupons and discovery routes; AliExpress metadata is a lead. Capture exact SKU/basket/destination, min spend/quantity, thresholds, expiration/time zone, membership/app/new-customer/coins/limited-redemption/payment conditions and whether totals were reproduced. Compare incompatible, sequential and conditional combinations; design future portal/cashback/gift-card/credit compatibility boundaries without accounts or integrations.

Starting context: [source audit](../audit.md), [domain proposal](../domain.md), [product brief](../brief.md), [slice](../vertical-slice.md). These are inputs, not accepted answers.

Additional input: [prior watch corpus, manifest and proposed cases](../watch-research-context.md). Use the source conflicts as investigation cases; do not accept the prior evidence labels or prices without reconciliation.

## Decision and acceptance gate

Produce a terms/evidence/compatibility proposal: verified application, conditional eligibility, unverified opportunity and absent verified coupon. Do not sum unsupported stacks or treat cashback as checkout cash. Define a safe later reproduction protocol; missing access/eligible coupon keeps success unproven rather than blocking the valid no-coupon path.

## Resolution comments

### 2026-10-27 — Resolution: accept the public-discount evidence and compatibility contract for planning

Accept the [primary-source research and compatibility proposal](../public-discounts-research.md) as the discount input for downstream planning. Parent review checked coverage against this ticket's gate and independently reopened eBay UK's generic coupon terms, WatchGecko's combination help and Shopify's discount-combination documentation. These establish terms and platform capabilities, not successful watch coupon applications or merchant-specific configuration.

Require attributed official terms and exact configuration/selected SKU, whole basket, quantity, UK destination, currency, eligibility, payment and observation-time evidence. Preserve threshold basis and strictness, caps, allocation, expiry/time zone, activation, limited redemption, channel/member/new-buyer/coin conditions and unknowns. AliExpress API/browser metadata and public banners remain leads. An advertised code alone cannot establish applicability or justify subtracting a discount from an already reduced price.

Accept four distinct states: verified application at observation time, conditional eligibility, unverified opportunity, and absent verified applicable public coupon within named checked routes. Eligibility, freshness, compatibility and reproduction retain separate evidence. Missing access or an eligible coupon leaves success unproven while permitting an ordinary no-coupon assessment under its own identity/cost gates; it does not establish exhaustive coupon absence.

Evaluate evidenced whole-scenario combinations, ordering, bases and allocations; unknown compatibility never grants permission. Compare incompatible alternatives rather than adding them, recheck shipping thresholds, count required filler items in basket commitment, and invalidate dependent totals/comparisons when context or terms change. Cashback remains a conditional later benefit separate from checkout payable; gift cards/credit are tender with acquisition-cost and refund context, never automatic free money. Future personal portals remain separately scoped adapters with no eligibility or integration assumed.

Accept the asset's bounded later reproduction protocol, stopping before purchase, payment commitment or benefit consumption and requiring separate authority for account/cart/address interaction. No exact watch basket, UK destination coupon application or discounted total was reproduced. Every retained cohort offer remains unqualified for a verified discounted delivered total, savings claim or alert; this resolution settles evidence requirements only.

The planning gate is met. No new sharp question requires a separate ticket: baseline policy owns comparable eligibility and usual-price claims; watch proof owns end-to-end application receipts; operating-envelope owns collection freshness/cost; portal-specific access remains existing fog. No implementation, account connection, purchase, infrastructure change, commit or push was performed.
