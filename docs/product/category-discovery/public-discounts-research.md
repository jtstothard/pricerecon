Research date: **2026-10-27**. Prepared for parent review of [public-discounts](tickets/public-discounts.md); no ticket or map status change. This is a planning contract, not implementation or an accepted live coupon application. **Zero baskets, coupon redemptions or checkout totals were reproduced.** Official public pages were opened live through web research; their text establishes advertised terms only. Search-index snippets were discovery aids, not application receipts. No account, subscription, linking, purchase, seller contact, infrastructure or production polling was used.

## Proposed terms and evidence contract

All following requirements are **P**, informed by the official examples above and the accepted identity/cost boundaries. They describe future evidence, not fields already available or a new service/schema.

An opportunity record must preserve issuer and exact host/store/seller namespace, campaign/code or automatic-discount identifier, official URL and minimal evidence span, observed UTC time, publication/update date when present, source route and access limits. Separate the literal term, extraction, interpretation and reproduction receipt. Preserve redacted evidence sufficient for review without saving account tokens, personal codes, addresses or payment details in the repo.

Bind any application to the accepted exact configuration and merchant variation: listing/PID **plus selected SKU/variant**, movement/revision/size/dial/strap, condition, fixed-price/auction/offer mode, stock and quantity. Include the **whole basket**, eligible and excluded lines, seller grouping, currency and unit/line amounts. “Quantity 1 watch” is a proposed test scenario until a selected basket confirms it. No filler item is free: compare its full basket cost and necessity, not an allocated watch price alone.

| Required terms/context | Required evidence and handling of unknowns |
| --- | --- |
| UK destination | Country GB plus real authorised postcode/region and service, distinguishing GB mainland and NI; public default England settings or GBP alone are insufficient. Keep full private address out of research records. Dispatch origin and consignment remain cost inputs. |
| Minimum/maximum | Amount, currency, strict/inclusive comparison, eligible lines and seller/order scope; pre/post other discounts; VAT-included/excluded and postage treatment; cap, rounding and allocation. Never reuse coupon minimum basis as customs intrinsic-value basis. |
| Quantity | Per-line/basket minimum, bundles, maximum units, per-customer/order limits, excluded variants/accessories and stock. Retain each quantity change as a new scenario. |
| Time | Start/end literal, named zone/UTC offset, inclusive/exclusive boundary, observed UTC and converted instant. Date without zone stays ambiguous; never assume London, UTC or Pacific time. Distinguish scheduled expiry from sold-out redemption quota. |
| Eligibility | Public versus targeted/member/subscriber, country/residency, age, new-buyer definition, prior purchase/account/device/payment restrictions; app/site channel/version; coin balance, required coin amount, conversion/cap and earning/activation requirements. Unknown is not eligible. Do not create accounts or claim rewards to manufacture proof. |
| Redemption | Claim/activation step, single-use/remaining allowance, campaign pool, eligible seller/category/SKU, promo field versus automatic application and exact rejection reason. No redemption attempt in this study. |
| Payment | Allowed method, billing region/currency, issuer/BIN or card-specific condition, minimum/cap, FX/fees, split tender and credit/gift-card treatment. A generally accepted payment method need not qualify for a coupon. |
| Reproduction | Before/after same-basket component totals, applied code labels and allocations, hidden automatic discounts displaced, shipping/tax/payment context, timestamp and exclusions. Terms and arithmetic must both agree; screenshot of a banner or success toast alone is insufficient. |

### Four application states, independently of cost completeness

1. **Verified application at observation time:** official terms and eligibility evidence agree with an exact selected basket/destination receipt showing the reduction and allocation. Scoped to that context/time; not a purchased outcome. Merchant coupon verification does not certify delivered cost or usual-price savings.
2. **Conditional eligibility:** terms identify an explicit unresolved eligibility/context assumption. Show the condition beside any hypothetical amount. This state does not assert an observed successful application; retain reproduction status separately.
3. **Unverified opportunity:** banner/metadata/code exists, but applicability, key terms, identity or reproduction is missing/conflicting. Keep outside verified totals and deal alerts. An expired or rejected code is a retained outcome with reason, not an active saving.
4. **Absent verified applicable public coupon in checked routes:** state routes/date and gaps. Means zero **evidenced immediate coupon deduction in the ordinary scenario**, not proof no coupon exists anywhere. A future verified no-coupon total still requires all identity/availability/cost gates.

Eligibility, terms freshness, compatibility and reproduction each retain their own status. Thus conditional eligibility cannot be promoted merely because a public code exists; an otherwise verified application becomes stale when its context changes. Public price and personal eligible alternatives remain separate comparison cohorts.

## Compatibility and arithmetic

**O:** [Shopify's platform documentation](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/discounts/discount-combinations) requires discounts to be configured to combine; supported capabilities do not prove a merchant enabled them. Product reductions precede order reductions on the revised subtotal; shipping discounts follow. Two shipping discounts cannot combine. Multiple percentage order reductions can use the same subtotal, so sequential percentage multiplication is not universal. No merchant's private settings were inspected.

**P:** represent compatibility for a whole scenario as supported, forbidden, conditional or unknown, with evidence and order/base rules. Pairwise permission is insufficient if the combined basket violates a cap, minimum or three-way rule. Enumerate alternatives rather than summing opportunities; silence is unknown, never permission.

| Combination case | Evidence-based/proposed result |
| --- | --- |
| eBay coupon + another coupon/rebate/promotion | Generic terms above forbid combination. Reject under those terms; inspect any campaign-specific variation before changing the rule. Do not generalize that all seller markdowns everywhere are forbidden. |
| WatchGecko two codes or code + offer | Official help forbids conjunction. Compare separate ordinary/code scenarios only where each is itself eligible; do not stack WATCHUSEEK and BOLDR code. A strap threshold does not qualify a watch-only basket. |
| Product discount then order discount | Platform supports this when configured. **Hypothetical:** £100 eligible item minus £20 product reduction gives £80; 10% order reduction on that revised base gives £72, before independently evidenced shipping/tax. £70 would incorrectly apply both to the original base. Not a live watch total. |
| Two order percentages | Do not assume the preceding algorithm: if both are evidenced as percentages of the same subtotal, hypothetical 10% + 20% on £100 gives £70; sequential 10% then 20% gives £72. Select only the observed documented rule. |
| AliExpress seller code + platform code + coins + app/new-buyer price | Conditional/unknown until campaign-specific terms and same-basket receipt prove eligibility, mutual compatibility, bases and allocation. No universal three-layer stack established. Coins already reflected in a display cannot be deducted twice. |
| Coupon + shipping threshold | Re-evaluate the shipping minimum after discounts according to merchant terms. A cheaper item subtotal can lose free delivery and produce a worse payable total. No guessed zero shipping. |
| Multi-item threshold/order allocation | Hypothetical £45 watch + £10 unwanted strap to qualify for £5 off costs £50 before shipping, not a £40 watch purchase. Record both line allocations and total basket commitment; returns can change eligibility. |
| Payment offer or cashback | Independently conditional; approval on checkout does not prove later issuer/portal payout. Compare payment fees and exclusions before an effective-cost scenario. |

Discounted immediate item/line/basket allocations feed the [UK-cost contract](uk-cost-research.md) once, with the correct gross/net basis. Reconcile source rounding and total; preserve conflicts. Do not invent tax refunds, subtract included VAT again, or assume crossing a coupon threshold settles consignment/import treatment. A lower displayed price is not a measured saving against usual obtainable price; history sufficiency remains separate.

## Future personal-benefit boundary

**O:** [TopCashback decline guidance](https://www.topcashback.co.uk/help/why-has-my-cashback-been-declined/) identifies unlisted voucher codes, returns/amendments, new-customer restrictions and last-referrer attribution as possible failure reasons. [Quidco claim guidance](https://www.quidco.com/article/make-a-claim/) identifies unapproved voucher/student codes and retailer-disallowed gift-card, store-credit or BNPL payment as possible claim blockers. These support a conditional boundary, not a rate or acceptance claim for any cohort merchant. No portal click-through, receipt upload, claim or integration was performed.

**P:** keep future TopCashback/Quidco benefits separate from checkout payable. Preserve eligible net base (including whether VAT/shipping excluded), rate/cap, attribution/session and approved-code rules, payment exclusions, return/validation period, and states opportunity → tracked → confirmed → payable → paid, plus declined/reversed. Tracking is not payment. Any hypothetical cashback-adjusted effective cost must show uncertainty and never replace the guaranteed payable amount or trigger a guaranteed delivered-budget alert.

Gift cards and account credit are tender, not automatic savings. Preserve face value, actual acquisition cost, fees, expiry, balance, merchant/currency restrictions, split-tender rules, refund destination and coupon/cashback compatibility. Do not subtract both face value and acquisition saving. Illustratively, spending a £100 gift card bought for £95 represents £95 acquisition cost allocated to that spend, not a £5 checkout discount plus £100 free money. Historical/sunk credit and refundable cash need distinct presentation. No purchase of discounted credit is proposed here.

Perks at Work, UNiDAYS and Blue Light remain **named future adapters with unresearched merchant-specific rules**. Capture eligibility attestation and its age, access/consent, private code scope, sharing restrictions, redemption limits and conflicts with public codes/portals. Do not infer eligibility, sign in, link accounts, expose private codes, create a generic portal scraper or assume student/member offers combine with cashback. Reuse the same scenario/evidence boundary; only separately authorised later research should specify portal access and maintenance.

## Correction, freshness and safe later reproduction

**P:** append corrections with issuer, evidence, reason, superseded claim and affected scenario IDs. Configuration changes, SKU mapping corrections, quantity/basket/destination/currency/payment changes, revised terms, expiry/quota failure, return/reversal or conflicting receipts invalidate dependent applications, totals, cohorts, rankings and alerts. Never transfer the wrong variant's successful coupon to the intended variant. Retain original observations without asserting they remain usable.

No primary evidence here establishes a universal freshness interval. Proposed freshness gate is the earliest known expiry, term/context change or source recheck requirement; dynamic stock, quotas and personal eligibility require a fresh pre-action check. Unknown timezone/end or unavailable terms precludes claiming current verified applicability. Acquisition failure means stale/unknown, not “coupon disappeared” or a fresh no-coupon result. Show observation time and checked coverage; recurring polling/retention policy belongs to its existing operating ticket.

Later reproduction requires a separately authorised bounded session, not execution under this asset:

1. Confirm permitted route/retention, exact merchant and configuration, purchase mode and stock. Agree one quantity/basket and actual UK destination with the buyer; distinguish NI. Obtain official campaign terms including all fields above. If login, private entitlement, app, coin activation or payment identity is needed, stop for separately scoped user handling; never create eligibility or bypass a gate.
2. Capture an ordinary same-context basket quote before applying the candidate, including any existing automatic reductions. No misleading “before” figure from RRP, another SKU, region or time. Obtain permission before cart/address interaction; none is authorised by this research.
3. In an isolated authorised session test one documented candidate, then only a bounded list of permitted combinations. Capture selections, codes, order of application, eligible base, line allocation, rejection/displacement messages, shipping/tax/payment totals, UTC and expiry zone. Removing a code must reveal whether a hidden automatic discount returns. Do not brute-force codes or exhaust limited redemptions/claim scarce benefits for a test.
4. Reconcile arithmetic against the quoted total and UK-cost components. Preserve a verified merchant subtotal separately if later imports/fees remain unknown. Record membership/app/new-buyer/coin/payment conditions without exporting personal details. Never infer final payment settlement from a quote.
5. Stop **before any order/payment/commitment button**; do not bid, submit Best Offers, redeem a gift card, activate credit, contact a seller or complete purchase. If applying a benefit itself consumes value or commits the user, stop before applying it and retain unverified status. Remove only test-owned cart state with authorisation; do not change the user's basket/profile. Record the exact stopping point and cleanup outcome.
6. If access fails, no eligible coupon exists, or verification would require excluded actions, retain the reason and complete the ordinary no-coupon assessment where its own gates pass. Coupon-success remains unproven; do not manufacture a success or discard the route's gap.
