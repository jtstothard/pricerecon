# Exact configuration and identity evidence: proposed contract

Research dated 2026-10-27 for [What constitutes an exact configuration and sufficient identity evidence?](tickets/identity-evidence.md). This is a planning contract and counterexample corpus, not a schema change, deployed resolver, or certification of current offers.

## Decision proposed

An **exact configuration** is a category-defined, versioned set of identity dimensions supported by attributable evidence. A source product ID, listing ID, SKU, title match or numeric confidence is a locator or claim, not that configuration. Keep three distinct results: **configuration identity**, **binding of a selected merchant option to that configuration**, and **qualification of the resulting offer for comparison**. Passing one never implies the others.

Use a complete-watch versus accessory gate before model matching. Require every category-mandatory dimension to be resolved, supported and nonconflicting before an exact configuration result. Then require a source-scoped purchasable variation and evidence binding its selected options to its price and availability before accepting an exact merchant offer. Destination, quantity, condition, purchase mode, freshness, eligibility and complete cost remain additional comparison gates owned by downstream decisions. “Exact” means supported configuration equivalence under a stated policy, not authenticated manufacture or guaranteed physical condition.

## Evidence classes and bounds

- **S — source inspected now:** working-tree code referenced below. Existing uncommitted connector changes were read, not changed or run. Source inspection establishes logic, not actual provider behavior.
- **R — retained receipt:** the [source decision](source-qualification-decision.md) and [watch corpus reconciliation](watch-research-context.md). Those assets own their dated observations and limitations; this research did not repeat merchant/browser/API probes or revalidate prices.
- **D — primary documentation read through web search:** official API/specification pages below, consulted 2026-10-27. These establish documented semantics or the issuer's own claims, not PriceRecon entitlement, actual selected offers, exhaustive inventory or archival permission.
- **P — proposal:** the contract, states and gates in this note. They are design choices derived from the counterexamples, not observed production behavior.

No accounts, carts, checkout, provider applications, runtime/device access, installs, commits, pushes or production polling were used. No new price series, merchant images or raw seller records were archived. The old corpus is used through its retained summary and manifest; original captures were not independently re-opened here. Therefore some cases deliberately resolve to unknown or conflict.

## Identifier authority and source traces

### AliExpress: three paths must remain distinguishable

**S:** [`_annotate_query_match`](../../../src/pricerecon/connectors/aliexpress.py) lowercases the title, checks inclusion of strong query tokens, and can set `exact_variant_confirmed=True` and HIGH confidence. This proves lexical agreement only. A title containing movement, dial or reference text can describe several available choices or an accessory; token inclusion does not bind the selected choice.

**S:** `_extract_ds_detail` in the same file takes `sku_list[0]` and flattens its sale/original price, stock and currency into product-level fields. The selection is positional, not an explicit requested-configuration lookup. Even a valid product response cannot make that first SKU the intended SKU. Alibaba's official DS page documents `product_id`, `ship_to_country`, currency and language parameters and marks the operation as requiring authorization; it does not establish that PriceRecon currently has DS entitlement. Its search-index text was readable; opening the full page timed out, so this note does not claim a freshly inspected complete DS response-field contract. [Alibaba DS product documentation](https://developer.alibaba.com/docs/api.htm?apiId=60452).

**S:** the staged browser-first path is stronger but different. [`BrowserOfferEvidence.qualification_issues`](../../../src/pricerecon/connectors/aliexpress_browser.py) requires a selected SKU, a price explicitly bound to it, casefold/trim equality for requested movement/dial/strap/size, GBP item price, GB destination and quantity one. `_search_browser_first` rejects capped discovery or any qualification issue before emitting PID:SKU listing IDs and `exact_variant_confirmed=True`. It leaves stock and landed total unknown. However, the predicate does not require maker, full reference, generation or complete-product/accessory classification. It is an offer-option binding check, not a complete category identity proof. A future implementation must name those results separately rather than consuming this Boolean as universal exactness.

**R:** the retained browser receipt changed an option from 2035 to VH31 and observed a changed displayed price and `data-sku-col`; no stable seller SKU or SKU-bound stock was established. Product JSON-LD InStock was generic. Therefore its outcome remains **selected-option observation, exact offer unresolved**. PID and DOM option-control IDs must not be relabelled as selected SKU IDs. [Source decision, AliExpress results](source-qualification-decision.md#aliexpress-public-destination-and-variant-controls).

**P:** use a source key containing AliExpress namespace, merchant identity when supplied, PID and stable selected SKU. Preserve option IDs and labels as source claims, plus the mapping evidence. Missing SKU means retain a lead/selection observation without a purchasable-variation identity. Do not invent one from an option label, title, first array element or URL.

### eBay: listing, variation, product and purchase mode

**D:** eBay's REST item ID distinguishes a single-SKU item (`v1|listing|0`) from a specific variation (`v1|listing|variation`). eBay also distinguishes a seller-created free-form SKU from the listing ID when converting legacy multi-variation items. Preserve the complete opaque item ID, not just its middle numeric component. [getItem documentation](https://edp.ebay.com/api-docs/buy/browse/resources/item/methods/getItem), [Browse legacy compatibility](https://developer.ebay.com/api-docs/buy/static/api-browse.html). Full getItem web opening exceeded the tool's content-size limit; the relevant indexed ID section and the shorter official overview were available. This is documented semantics, not a new live item-detail call.

**D:** FIXED_PRICE, AUCTION and BEST_OFFER are distinct buying options; an auction may also offer Buy It Now, while a negotiable offer is not an obtained sale price. Broad NEW/USED filtering does not establish granular condition. [eBay filter reference](https://edp.ebay.com/api-docs/buy/ref-buy-browse-filters.html). Preserve provider condition ID, raw label, description and any structured descriptors; eBay documents these as separate fields and describes free-text condition as seller-provided. [eBay condition field definitions](https://www.developer.ebay.com/api-docs/buy/feed/v1/schema/item_all_active). These Feed definitions corroborate field meaning; they do not qualify Feed access or substitute for Browse receipts.

**S:** [`_parse_listings`](../../../src/pricerecon/connectors/ebay.py) keeps the returned `itemId` but leaves condition and variant fields empty and does not retain buying options in `NormalizedListing`. It also defaults absent price/currency and availability values. This transport cannot by itself establish a granular condition cohort or a fixed-price purchasable offer. No parser fix is made here.

**R:** the source ticket's detail probes distinguish exact Casio suffix, a different green suffix, used Good with missing exact reference, and used Excellent with a different reference. Fixed price/best offer and condition fields were observed in raw details, but that evidence is not automatically present in the normalized listing. [Retained eBay receipt](source-qualification-decision.md#ebay-exact-reference-and-used-market-separation).

**P:** scope identity by source and full variation-aware item ID; retain marketplace and seller separately. An EPID/catalogue association or GTIN can support a candidate product mapping but does not certify the seller's actual physical item or selected variation. Condition and buying mode are offer dimensions, not a rewrite of the manufacturer product identity.

### Shopify: a platform is not a merchant

**D:** Shopify's ProductVariant exposes an ID, parent product, selected option name/value pairs, optional SKU/barcode, price and availability. Parent product availability can mean at least one variant is available. `variantBySelectedOptions` can return null; a first-available fallback is different from a successful requested-option match. [Shopify ProductVariant and related product fields](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/storefront/2026-01/objects/ProductVariant).

**D:** Shopify documents `?variant=[variant-id]` deep links and requires displayed media, price and selector to reflect the chosen variant. A parent product link may fall back to a first-available variant. [Shopify variant deep-link guidance](https://shopify.dev/docs/storefronts/themes/product-merchandising/variants).

**S:** [`ShopifyConnector`](../../../src/pricerecon/connectors/shopify.py) uses connector ID `shopify` for every storefront, per-variant IDs (or handle fallback), parent-product links without selected variant, title-level product identity, and extracted specifications from title plus variant title. Store base URL is retained as seller text. SKU, barcode, structured option mapping and canonical storefront identity are not preserved as an identity contract. Missing price/currency defaults are downstream qualification risks, not accepted free/GBP offers.

**P:** namespace source locators by a stable merchant/storefront key plus parent product and variant IDs. Preserve observed domain and verified aliases separately; “Shopify”, a shop title and “official store” text do not establish manufacturer ownership. A handle-only fallback identifies a product page, not a variation. Keep a variant deep link when supported, then verify returned selection; constructing a URL is not evidence that it selected the right option.

### GTIN, MPN and reference aliases

**D:** GS1's GTIN standard governs trade-item identification, but its General Specifications also allow consumer-product variations under a GTIN when a new GTIN is not required. Thus a GTIN alone cannot be assumed to encode every preference-relevant distinction. [GS1 General Specifications, consumer product variants](https://ref.gs1.org/standards/genspecs/21.0.1/). This cited release is older; the narrow rule demonstrates the counterexample and is not presented as a complete current compliance assessment. Declared pack-count changes are specifically addressed by the GTIN management rules. [GS1 declared net content](https://www.gs1.org/1/gtinrules/en/rule/266/declared-net-content).

**P:** store identifier type, issuing namespace, literal value, normalization rule, issuer/evidence and effective time. Verify aliases rather than globally stripping punctuation or regional suffixes. MPNs are manufacturer-scoped; seller SKUs are merchant-scoped. A valid checksum is syntax evidence, not evidence that the seller attached the identifier correctly. Region aliases require an explicit supported mapping; preserve original values so mistaken merges can be undone.

## Proposed claim and resolution contract

Each claim needs a stable claim identity; subject (family, exact configuration, source product, source variation, specific used item or offer); field and unit; literal assertion and normalized candidate; issuer and issuer role; origin URL/document locator; observed time and source-effective time when known; route; precise text span/JSON path/image-region locator; capture identity or permitted minimal evidence pointer; and derivation links. Include source terms/retention classification rather than assuming every capture may be kept. Preserve unknown timestamps as unknown.

Separate the **issuer** from the **extractor**. A seller specification extracted by AI is still a seller assertion; the AI supplies a derived extraction with model/run, input claim IDs and uncertainty. A copied manufacturer sentence is not a new manufacturer statement unless its origin is traced. Repeated extraction of one page, mirrored catalogue text and repeated AI responses share their original evidence lineage and do not count as independent corroboration.

Authority is field-specific, not one global source ranking:

| Field or claim | Preferred support and conflict treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Intended manufacturer's reference, declared dimensions, movement or revision | Manufacturer specification for the exact reference/revision is the primary definition when actually attributable. A reseller page is the reseller's claim. Conflicting definitions remain visible; do not borrow a specification from a nearby family. |
| What this merchant will sell | Selected merchant variation/options, price and availability evidence at a stated time. Manufacturer specification cannot select the merchant SKU. A title/JSON-LD/selector conflict blocks binding until reconciled. |
| Used item's condition, included links/box/papers, modifications | Seller statement and item-specific evidence, kept separate from catalogue defaults. Photos may support visible scratches or apparent colour, with limits; stock images cannot establish the actual item's condition. |
| Extracted or inferred value | Preserve input and transformation, never promote the extractor to authority. Explicitly distinguish visible observation, inference and unsupported assumption. |
| Human correction | Named correction of the exact claim/mapping, rationale and evidence or explicit personal observation. It can select a user's preference or adjudicate a mapping; it cannot create missing provider stock, entitlement, physical authenticity or checkout proof. |

A manufacturer document describes an intended product. A seller claims what is offered. Neither alone proves the received object is authentic or that its movement is healthy. This limitation is a proposed product claim boundary: no image classifier or repeated claim can establish invisible internals, service history or authenticity from these inputs.

Use categorical resolution states with reasons and evidence IDs: **unresolved** (mandatory field absent), **candidate** (plausible family/configuration), **supported** (every required identity dimension supported without unresolved material conflict), **conflicting** (incompatible relevant claims), **rejected** (known mismatch/accessory), **superseded** (historical claim or mapping replaced), and **needs revalidation** (support/binding no longer usable). Optional numeric confidence may order review candidates, but never bypasses required fields or conflicts. Distinguish unknown, explicitly not applicable and absent-from-capture; absence is not a match wildcard.

## Corrections and invalidation

1. Append a correction event; retain permitted original evidence and the prior mapping. Record who corrected which field/mapping, why, supporting claim IDs, scope and time. Never silently rewrite a past observation to appear as if the correct variant was originally selected.
2. Separate correcting extraction (the page said mint, parser read gray), correcting a source claim (seller contradicted by supported reference), and correcting user intent (the buyer now wants gray). These have different subjects and effects. A preference change does not rewrite product facts.
3. Compute affected dependencies from claim/mapping IDs and policy version: selected-variant binding, cohorts, historical aggregates, cost scenarios, discounts, rankings and alerts. Mark dependent outputs invalid/needs-revalidation until recomputed. If the wrong variation was priced, retain it only as that variation if support exists; otherwise exclude it, rather than moving its price to the intended variant.
4. Keep an assessment's as-of evidence and version. Recomputed outputs show that the basis changed; prior notifications are not erased. Suppress new definitive deal alerts while a required dependency conflicts. Whether to send a correction notification is an experience/alert-policy decision; this contract requires traceability, not a second notification system.
5. A source edit, changed selected option, identifier reuse/alias discovery, removed evidence or policy revision can invalidate a current mapping. Re-observation does not extend authenticity, physical-health or terms permission. Exact retention windows, replay/storage mechanics and stale-history policy belong to the observation-history ticket.

## Shared and category boundary

The shared boundary owns namespaced locators, claims and derivation lineage, resolution states/reasons, evidence/policy versions, human correction events and dependency invalidation. Category policy owns required identity dimensions, alias/equivalence rules, unit semantics, product-kind exclusions and meaningful condition descriptors. Existing `Watch` continues to mean tracked search per [CONTEXT.md](../../../CONTEXT.md); use “wristwatch” for the product category.

For wristwatches require maker and full reference (or an explicitly supported reference-less configuration), generation/revision when material, case size with dimension axis, movement, date/complication distinction, dial/finish, strap/bracelet and complete-product status. Require relevant included links/components and modifications at offer level. An unknown generation is acceptable only where supported evidence establishes no unresolved generation distinction for the mapping; it is never a wildcard across known revisions.

For PC parts the same mechanism accepts category keys for manufacturer part number, model/generation, capacity/configuration, form factor/interface when identity-defining, complete component/system, kit topology and bundle contents. For pens it accepts model/edition, writing system, nib grade/material, finish, filling mechanism and complete pen versus component. These stress tests establish that the shared contract has no wristwatch-only fields; they do not close detailed PC or pen taxonomy decisions.

Do not introduce a new evidence service, scheduler or storage platform through this decision. [`NormalizedListing`](../../../src/pricerecon/models/listings.py) provides useful transport fields but lacks the proposed field lineage and resolution semantics; consuming its Boolean or loose variant dictionary as this contract would be an implementation gap. Schema/migration/reuse design remains outside this planning effort.

## Acceptance gate and remaining limits

The research gate is met as a **proposed contract with explicit abstention cases**: source identity scopes and code paths are traced; all retained cohort cases have required outcomes; positive manufacturer-reference support contrasts with unresolved selections; claims, conflicts and correction consequences are explicit; PC and pen cases challenge category separation. It does not require making the unavailable Merkur or unsigned Militado evidence magically available.

Before any future resolver is accepted, require executable fixtures or an agreed disposable review artifact demonstrating: title-token and first-SKU false positives rejected; a correctly mapped full-reference variation supported; product-kind accessory rejected; family/PID-only selection unresolved; missing dimension not treated as wildcard; conflicting dial/revision blocked; Shopify storefront/variant separation; eBay complete variation ID, condition and buying mode retained; duplicated AI lineage not independent; correction removes the wrong observation from dependent results; and unknown physical authenticity/health stays unknown. These are future implementation acceptance gates, not tests run in this session.

No complete current merchant offer has passed identity plus stock, UK cost, freshness and eligibility gates here. Downstream history, UK costing and public discount investigations can proceed using this contract while retaining those unknowns. Source permissions/access and retention prerequisites from the source decision remain active. The existing PC-generalisation and pen fog cover later category detail; no extra ticket is needed merely to restate them.
