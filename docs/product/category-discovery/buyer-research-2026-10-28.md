## Buying goal and aesthetic criteria

Goal, in the buyer's words: “find my next watch”. They want playful, nerdy,
sophisticated designs that express an adult's inner child. Integrated use of the
watch's form, dial and hands matters. The Fossil Gengar design and Avatar ×
Holzkern were positive references; a character silhouette applied as an afterthought
was a negative reference. The buyer describes the Pokémon G-Shock as feeling too
much like a toy, while also being interested in bold, toy-like artistic forms that
feel well made rather than cheap/plasticky.

The buyer identifies as non-binary/genderqueer and prefers unusual design outside
conventional gender norms. They find many typical “men's” designs unappealing and
are more comfortable with femme/strange designs. Their concrete dislikes include
large dials, black/white palettes and technical/sporty clutter or poor readability.
This is self-described taste, not permission to infer preferences from identity
or impose a binary product-gender filter.

Important interviewer correction: the buyer does **not** perceive their 787B
concept as busy. They describe a distinctive shape, bold artistic pattern and
some indices. Artistic pattern is not equivalent to technical dial clutter.

The buyer invited inspection of their own Politoed and 787B concepts in the
3d-printing repo. Those were read as design references only, not treated as
purchasable offers or real specifications for the fixture cohort.

## Fit, budget and information order

- Size concerns are a dial/case that makes their wrist look small and a watch
  that stands too far off the wrist. Current G-Shock GW-3000B feels much too big;
  Edifice EQW-M1000D is approximately the largest they would consider. They would
  not buy those older designs now. No numerical diameter/thickness limit was
  supplied or inferred from model specifications.
- Normal collection-addition budget: roughly **GBP 100–150**. Up to **GBP 300** is
  an exception for a particularly desirable design, including Gengar/Seiko/BOLDR
  Pokémon examples. No purchasing authority is granted by these amounts.
- After visual interest, first details are price, materials and mechanism.
- No manual-wind watch. Automatic is possible, with thickness checked immediately.
  Reliability and a known **movement manufacturer** matter; this was explicitly
  clarified as the movement maker, not necessarily the watch brand.
- Vegan preference: avoid new leather/other animal products where possible. Used
  animal-derived materials are more acceptable, and swapping a strap is possible.
  This is not a categorical exclusion of every watch with a leather strap.
- Crystal/durability matters because of scratching and bumping during hikes and
  ordinary public wear. No specific crystal type, water-resistance rating or
  technical durability threshold was selected.
- Missing specs are Googled. An official source or multiple independent reviewers
  provides confidence. Count independent evidence, not duplicated/cross-posted claims.

## Discovery, saving and similarity

The buyer discovered Gengar while browsing Pokémon Center and also encounters
watches in Instagram ads. They saw Gengar above GBP 1000, searched other sources
and possible restocks, then abandoned it as unlikely to become reasonably priced.
They did not maintain a wishlist/tracker, but would prefer to keep track of watches
that interest them.

Saving a watch should monitor that item and continue finding similar alternatives.
For Gengar, similarity means similarly thoughtful integration of **the buyer's own
special interests**, not an arbitrary well-integrated theme they do not follow.
Known starting interests: Pokémon, Avatar, F1, Firefly, Star Wars and Marvel.
The list is explicitly incomplete and should remain editable. These are expressed
interests, not validated product tags or evidence of an official licensed collaboration.
Other creative, colourful designs can still appeal; themed similarity is not a
hard requirement for every watch in discovery.

## Alerts and price-benchmark intent

Requested events:

- A significant bargain/price reduction.
- A retail restock.
- The watch reaching the buyer's chosen price.
- Relevant similar watches, with configurable routing.

For a significant bargain the buyer specified **20% or more**, measured against
average prices **actually paid**. When reliable completed-sale data is sparse or
missing, they want average listing/asking prices as a fallback. The interface must
state which basis is used; an asking-price estimate is not a completed-sale average.
The buyer did not choose an estimator, time window, sample size, cohort rules,
freshness policy, outlier treatment or data-use rights. This request does not
supply empirical calibration or authorize a fabricated usual-price/savings claim.
Current unavailable-baseline gates remain valid until a qualified method/data
contract exists.

Channels: in-app, Telegram and Discord. Which events interrupt externally versus
appear in-app should be **configurable**. Granularity, defaults, quiet hours,
digests and repeat thresholds were not settled. Do not infer a fixed routing rule
from the interview or activate any external delivery.
