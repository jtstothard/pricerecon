# Product brief

## Users and jobs

Confirmed by the user during charting: the first useful release is single-user, for their own UK buying decisions. The buyer chooses among comparable products and offers. Operators must understand source failures and ongoing cost. A public audience changes eligibility, licensing and operational requirements.

Retain all three jobs:

1. Specific item: “Tell me when this exact watch is below £60 delivered.”
2. Preference discovery: “Find compact, colourful watches under £150.” Preferences must become reviewable constraints; “compact” and “colourful” need definitions, with unknown specifications visible.
3. Category discovery: “Show unusually good watch deals, including products I was not tracking.” Discovery cannot depend solely on a pre-existing item watch.

Watches prove the complete workflow. PC parts challenge generalisation next. Fountain pens remain intended scope, without pretending their taxonomy is already designed.

## Outcomes

A buyer can see the exact configuration, condition, merchant, evidence age, UK total and eligibility; understand why an offer is unusual or why evidence is insufficient; follow the offer; correct identity or preference interpretation; and track or receive alerts without losing today's query monitoring. An operator can see supported coverage, failed or stale sources, collection gaps and costs.

Comprehensive discovery means systematic discovery across a declared, supported source set and observable query/category/merchant coverage. It does not mean every retailer, page or offer was checked. Distinguish caps, pagination, excluded routes, blocked sources, unsupported merchants and time of last check.

## Scope retained

AliExpress explicitly; eBay and relevant second-hand sellers; brand/manufacturer-direct; UK retailers; international retailers shipping to the UK; search engines, shopping aggregators and deal communities as discovery leads. A lead is not a verified merchant offer.

Expose usual obtainable price from repeated comparable available offers, current best comparable offer within checked coverage, observed historical low with date and eligibility, and personal eligible price. RRP and struck-through prices may be labelled seller claims but cannot establish deal value. New products with insufficient history are potential deals with limited evidence, without invented savings.

Public coupon discovery, conditions, reproduction and compatible stacking are initial scope where available; no coupon is a valid complete path. Design future boundaries for Perks at Work, UNiDAYS, Blue Light, TopCashback, Quidco, discounted gift cards and account credit. Keep guaranteed checkout costs separate from uncertain cashback and payment scenarios.

## Non-goals of this effort

No implementation or deployment, account integration, infrastructure installation, purchase, external publication or third-party contact. No universal coverage promise, statistical baseline formula chosen before evidence, automated authenticity certification, or public-service launch assumption. The four proposed work areas are lenses on one dependency graph, not four builds.

## Success and acceptance

The [watch slice](vertical-slice.md) supplies normal, no-coupon and unresolved cases. Planning succeeds when each unresolved decision has an owner/claim mechanism, required evidence and an acceptance gate. Product usefulness must later be judged with a buyer, not inferred from mocked connector tests or ticket closure.
