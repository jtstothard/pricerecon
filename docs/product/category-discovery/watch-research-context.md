## What is available

`outputs/watch-catalogue-data.json` contains **94 watch entries**. `outputs/hermes-watch-prices.csv` and `outputs/watch-price-benchmarks.csv` each contain **417 rows**; these are related raw/annotated presentations, not 834 independent observations. The benchmark JSON describes its source as “Hermes 417 records; reviewed with corrections.” The report's evidence counts total 417, but those evidence labels are prior author classifications, not fresh live verification.

The corpus includes candidate specifications, merchant and discovery links, images/image-source attribution, price/FX/eligibility fields, corrections, buyer-oriented decisions, HTML/PDF previews and saved page extracts. I inspected structured data, notes and one saved browser JSON example; I did not visually validate the previews or inspect every page capture. No connector, merchant, checkout, currency rate or tax rule was verified live in this follow-up.

The pricing report explicitly says observations are not independently checkout-verified. The original catalogue guide describes broad GBP bands as editorial planning estimates and 11.11 prices as unconfirmed. A later supplement suspends vegan restrictions for that research; older notes still contain vegan exclusions. Treat chronological updates as research-context changes, not an instruction to enforce old filters in PriceRecon. Retained preferences concern small-wrist fit, legibility, colour and artistic dials. The 145–149 mm wrist figure is explicitly a retained estimate, not a newly verified measurement. Import these as editable preference candidates for buyer review, not universal category requirements.

## Evidence pitfalls exposed by the corpus

- SN0151 catalogue specifications describe a 39 mm automatic while the selected benchmark URL points to GS, and accompanying notes distinguish GS 32.5 mm quartz from GL/GL-S 39 mm automatic. Keep it as a configuration-conflict case; do not inherit the cheapest family's price.
- AD2095 notes say “From USD 65” spans VK64 / 0S21 options with selected-variant price unverified. The notes also contain IOSS-related tax wording: it is an unverified prior claim, not an accepted UK tax rule. The UK-cost investigation must consult current authoritative UK guidance.
- Saved/merged fields can disagree: confidence text sometimes retains old findings and appended corrections while structured `variant`, `selected` or display-price fields still reflect another configuration. More repeated text does not create independent confirmation. Corrections need field-level lineage and dependent assessment invalidation.
- “Live-selected-listing,” seller “official” names, “delivered,” and buying judgements in prior notes are author claims until reconciled with original captures and exact destination/eligibility. Contemporary screenshots/extracts may support particular past observations; they do not establish today's availability, a working PriceRecon connector, recurring obtainable prices, or permission to reuse images publicly.
- One-day multi-channel surveying is valuable discovery coverage, not a longitudinal usual-price baseline. Predictions about 11.11/Black Friday and seller strike-throughs must not become measured discounts.
