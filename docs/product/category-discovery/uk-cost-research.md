# Evidence for a defensible UK delivered total

Research for [UK cost](tickets/uk-cost.md), checked **2026-10-27**. Proposed planning contract for parent review; no ticket resolution, tax engine, checkout reproduction or current offer qualification. Only this asset was written. Existing dirty work was preserved. Public primary-source browsing and local source inspection only; no accounts, operational access, basket, purchase, commit or branch operation.

## Finding

A delivered total is defensible only for a bound exact offer, quantity, destination, dispatch route and payment scenario, with every buyer-payable component either evidenced or explicitly inapplicable. A price in GBP, a marketplace VAT policy, or `price + shipping` does not meet that gate. Keep the amount payable to the merchant distinct from additional border/carrier liabilities and payment costs. Missing evidence produces a conditional calculation or unknown total, never an implicit zero.

## Verified source rules and their limits

All links below were checked on the research date. Rules concern a private consumer buying ordinary non-excise goods; business VAT numbers, gifts, excise products, returned-goods relief and unusual customs procedures require separate evidence. Do not apply gift allowances to purchased watches.

### Destination, location and consignment

**HMRC marketplace rules:** low-value goods outside the UK sold to GB consumers attract supply VAT at sale; goods outside both UK and EU sold to NI consumers attract import VAT through the marketplace rules. The £135 ceiling is for the imported consignment. UK-located stock sold by an overseas business can also make the marketplace liable for VAT, regardless of value. Above £135 normal import rules apply. EU→NI has separate rules. [HMRC overseas marketplace guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/vat-and-overseas-goods-sold-to-customers-in-the-uk-using-online-marketplaces) (updated 13 May 2022).

**Valuation:** intrinsic value is the selling price excluding separately identified transport/insurance and identifiable taxes/charges; transport embedded in the price without separate invoice disclosure remains included. Aggregate all items in a consignment unless sent individually. Exactly £135 belongs to the low-value branch; £135.01 does not. Quantity and seller changes can cross the boundary and require VAT adjustment. This threshold basis is different from the import-tax calculation base. [HMRC marketplace charging guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/charging-vat-when-goods-are-sold-if-youre-an-online-marketplace-operator) (updated 20 June 2025).

**Import charges:** import VAT includes goods, postage/packing/insurance and duty. GB non-excise consignments at or below £135 have no customs duty; higher-value duty depends on goods and origin. NI requires a separate at-risk decision: current consumer guidance specifies €3 per item, converted to sterling, for non-business recipients of at-risk consignments at or below £135; above it EU rates apply. Not-at-risk NI consignments use the low-value relief/UK-rate route. Carrier confirmation is needed; do not infer risk status from a postcode. [GOV.UK tax and duty](https://www.gov.uk/goods-sent-from-abroad/tax-and-duty).

**Proposed policy:** record destination nation (England/Scotland/Wales/NI), relevant postcode/region and selected service; country code `GB` alone is insufficient to resolve NI treatment or remote-area postage. Separate seller establishment, goods' physical location at sale, dispatch country, manufacturing/customs origin, and intermediate routing. GBP, a `.co.uk` domain or a UK seller address proves none of these. UK stock should not acquire the consumer's fictitious overseas-import bill. EU stock is not UK stock for GB; EU→NI must not use the GB import branch. GB→NI or unclear movements require their own route evidence rather than extrapolation from the outside-UK-and-EU rule.

A basket is not necessarily a consignment: retain seller grouping, quantities, consolidation/split evidence and changes. Do not promise that two £80 items avoid the threshold because each is below it, or assert consolidation solely because they share a checkout. If grouping is unknown near the ceiling, show alternative scenarios and withhold a verified total. Discounts must have an attributable allocation; gift cards and payment credit do not automatically reduce customs value.

### Duty classification and carrier fees

The official tariff requires product details such as materials, use and manufacture to identify the commodity code and rates. [UK Trade Tariff entry guidance](https://www.gov.uk/trade-tariff). A watch-family title is insufficient: complete watch versus movement/strap, case material, movement/display and applicable tariff measures need evidence. Preserve classification basis, tariff date, destination regime, origin and any preferential-origin proof. Dispatch through the EU does not itself prove EU preferential origin. Missing classification/origin means unknown duty, not a generic watch percentage or zero. No specific watch code/rate was qualified in this research.

Royal Mail's checked receiving page lists customs handling charges of **£8 for Royal Mail products, £12 for Parcelforce products**, and £25 for goods over £900 requiring a full declaration. These are carrier/service-specific, not a universal import surcharge. [Royal Mail fee explanation](https://www.royalmail.com/receiving-mail/pay-a-fee). Capture the actual carrier, service, applicable schedule, fee tax treatment and whether the seller already pays it. A delivery-duty-paid/all-import-charges-included commitment needs offer-specific scope; it is not established by free shipping. Unknown courier or disbursement fees stay unknown. Do not add a handling fee to every VAT-inclusive low-value order.

### Marketplace evidence

**eBay:** its first-party policy says VAT is collected on certain UK orders, including imports up to £135 and UK-located goods from non-UK businesses; VAT appears in the checkout total, with tax invoices available after purchase. Imports above £135 may require recipient payment during clearance. The policy also warns tax treatment depends on item location, destination and value, and buyer service fees can exist. [eBay tax policy](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/default/default/tax-policy?id=4348). This documents platform behavior, not collection on an inspected listing, and its general UK wording does not override HMRC's NI distinctions. Require destination/selected-variation-specific price, postage, tax and any buyer fees; preserve whether VAT is included or added and what the indication covers. An empty tax array is unknown; a VAT-included flag alone does not settle shipping, import liabilities or payment fees.

**AliExpress:** first-party EU/EEA/UK transaction terms §§4.2–4.3 distinguish third-party charges, taxes/duties and financial charges, and provide for collection when legally required. Section 6.2 says an absent profile address can lead to an assigned ship-to location based on subdomain, IP or browsing history, with corresponding prices. [AliExpress platform transaction terms](https://cdn.contract.alibaba.com/terms/EU_EE_UK_platform_service_agreement/20250514112355975/20250514112355975.html). These terms do not prove that every UK product display includes VAT or that a particular checkout collected it. Searches for official UK £135 help material did not yield a verifiable UK-specific collection article; third-party calculators were excluded. Apply HMRC rules as rules, but keep actual AliExpress collection/inclusion unknown until a permitted exact-offer receipt supports it. Do not label UK VAT collection “IOSS”: the retained corpus's IOSS wording is not accepted UK tax evidence.

### VAT rate, shipping and currencies

The current UK standard VAT rate is **20%**, applying to most goods/services; reduced, zero and exempt treatments also exist. [GOV.UK VAT rates](https://www.gov.uk/vat-rates). Use the applicable product/service treatment, not 20% for every possible category. For an amount already wholly subject to included 20% VAT, its VAT portion is gross ÷ 6; adding another 20% double-counts it. This is arithmetic under a specified treatment, not evidence that a display is VAT-inclusive.

**Proposed shipping policy:** bind destination, quantity, selected warehouse and service to a shipping quote, including compulsory packing/insurance and region surcharges. “Free shipping” supports zero only within its evidenced conditions. Keep optional insurance/express upgrades as separate scenarios. A search snippet, generic merchant shipping page or free-over-minimum policy without basket qualification cannot establish the selected offer's postage. Allocate shared basket shipping transparently; a per-watch allocation is not the standalone obtainable total.

HMRC customs currency conversion has its own rules, generally using the applicable published rate when the declaration is accepted, with specified contractual exceptions. It is not necessarily the rate paid by the buyer. [HMRC customs exchange-rate guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/customs-valuation/exchange-rates) (updated 14 April 2026), [official monthly rate service](https://www.trade-tariff.service.gov.uk/exchange_rates/monthly). No numerical current FX rate was adopted here.

PayPal documents a transaction exchange rate incorporating a conversion fee; where a card issuer converts, that issuer sets the rate and any fees. [PayPal exchange-rate explanation](https://securepayments.paypal.com/uk/cshelp/article/where-can-i-find-paypals-currency-calculator-and-exchange-rates-help109), [PayPal UK agreement, currency conversion choices](https://www.paypal.com/uk/webapps/mpp/ua/useragreement-full). Therefore preserve displayed currency, actual charge currency, funding currency, converter, rate direction/time/expiry, spread already embedded and any separate fee. Do not add a second spread to a rate that already includes it. A GBP merchant charge may avoid a conversion in a GBP funding scenario; it does not establish every card's fee schedule. A spot GBP equivalent is an estimate, not a guaranteed debit. Keep HMRC valuation conversion and buyer-payment conversion in separate traces, especially near £135.

## Proposed cost component and status contract

This is policy for review, not an implemented schema. Each scenario references the accepted configuration/binding and correction versions; source/store/listing/variation, condition and purchase mode; quantity and basket/consignment membership; location/origin/destination; timestamp, access route and permitted evidence pointer; price eligibility and selected shipping/payment route. An auction bid or unaccepted best offer is not an obtainable fixed purchase price.

For every component retain: amount (decimal) or absent, explicit currency, quantity/basis, inclusion relationship, payer/collector, evidence and observation time, assumptions, validity conditions and status. Distinguish **observed/quoted**, **derived under stated rules**, **conditional**, **unknown**, **conflicting**, and **not applicable**. An observed seller assertion remains an assertion with its issuer; it is not a customs ruling. A zero requires evidence or a supported non-applicability reason. Record taxes as included, added, not applicable or unknown, not one ambiguous `tax=0`.

| Component | Evidence and accounting rule |
| --- | --- |
| Item subtotal | Selected configuration and quantity, unit versus line price, explicit currency; no first-SKU/from-price substitution. |
| Immediate discount | Reproduced applicable reduction and allocation; preserve gross/net basis. Coupon opportunity alone cannot lower the payable total. |
| Shipping/packing/insurance | Destination/service/basket-bound quote; mark already included charges to avoid duplication. |
| Merchant/marketplace VAT | Included amount is disclosure within gross, never an additional summand; separately added VAT contributes once. Retain collector and coverage. |
| Duty | Applicable classification/origin/regime and valuation, or exact covered seller commitment; unknown cannot be zero. |
| Import VAT still payable | Applicable import base/rate and evidence it is not already settled/covered. Do not subtract a guessed “VAT credit” or assume a refund. |
| Carrier clearance/disbursement | Actual carrier/service schedule or bill, payer and inclusion; no universal £8 assumption. |
| Other compulsory buyer fees | Marketplace/service charges and applicable tax, from selected route. |
| Payment/FX | Actual charge currency and chosen conversion/fee context, distinct from customs valuation. |
| Deferred benefits | Cashback, points and contingent rebates excluded from guaranteed payable total; separate uncertain effective-cost view. Credit/gift-card face value is a payment source, not automatically a discount. |

Arithmetic: **merchant payable = selected item lines − evidenced immediate discounts + separately payable shipping/services + separately added tax/fees**. **Delivered scenario = merchant payable + additional buyer-payable duty/import VAT/carrier charges + applicable payment costs**. Included amounts are counted once. Components in different currencies require their own attributable conversion before addition. Retain full precision and show rounding basis; reconcile to source totals rather than silently forcing a match. Unreconciled discrepancies are conflicts.

Total semantics:

- **Verified at observation time:** exact offer/destination/quantity/payment context reproduced with evidence for every payable component or supported inapplicability, no material conflicts or unbounded later liabilities. Means a reviewable current quoted total, not a purchased/settled outcome or guarantee against seller/customs error. A checkout total alone may be only a verified merchant payable subtotal. For recipient-paid imports, final delivered cost may remain conditional until assessment/billing.
- **Conditional:** complete arithmetic under explicit unresolved assumptions/eligibility or rate/fee alternatives. Show each condition adjacent to the amount. A bounded range needs evidence for both bounds. It cannot be relabelled verified because the arithmetic is exact.
- **Unknown:** a material price/currency/shipping/tax/duty/fee or identity/context input is missing, conflicting or unbounded. Show known payable subtotal and missing components; no single delivered figure. A lower bound is permitted only if all remaining contributions and reductions are constrained so it really is a bound.

A verified public scenario and a personal conditional payment/coupon scenario can coexist. Corrections to configuration, quantity, consignment, destination, dispatch, tax inclusion, coupon allocation or payment assumptions invalidate dependent totals/rankings/alerts. Require revalidation when source context changes; no evidence-derived universal freshness interval was established here.

## Hypothetical arithmetic exercises

**Every amount, checkout label, carrier assignment and FX rate below is invented solely to exercise the cited rules. None is a live offer, checkout capture, tariff classification or current exchange rate.** All examples assume an exact complete non-excise watch configuration, private consumer, fixed-price sale and applicable 20% VAT unless stated. The evidence needed for an actual verified result is described explicitly.

| Case | Arithmetic and required result |
| --- | --- |
| GB low-value marketplace, VAT already included | Assume China→England, quantity 1, separately invoiced net goods £100 and net delivery £5; exact checkout shows goods £120 + delivery £6, all including 20% VAT. Intrinsic value £100; merchant payable **£126**, including VAT £21. Do not add £25.20. With supported GB low-value treatment, no buyer import/clearance liability and GBP payment with no separate fee, £126 could qualify as verified only with those actual receipts. Here it is hypothetical. [Valuation](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/charging-vat-when-goods-are-sold-if-youre-an-online-marketplace-operator), [VAT rate](https://www.gov.uk/vat-rates). |
| Exact boundary versus quantity | Assume GB destination and separately shown £10 net transport. Goods intrinsic **£135** stay in low-value treatment even though goods+transport £145; at 20% fully taxable checkout, £145×1.20 = **£174**. Goods £135.01 cross the ceiling. Two £80 net watches sent together have intrinsic £160, not £80, and require import treatment; two genuinely separate consignments need dispatch evidence. Embedded unseparated freight cannot simply be deducted to get below £135. [Consignment rule](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/charging-vat-when-goods-are-sold-if-youre-an-online-marketplace-operator). |
| GB import over threshold, duty and carrier uncertain | Assume net goods £200 + shipping £20; no checkout VAT, seller does not cover imports. Let actual duty be D and carrier charge H: import VAT = 20%×(£220+D), delivered = **£264 + 1.20D + H**. If merely assuming D=£10 and H=£8 gives £284, that is conditional, not a watch duty rate determination. With unknown classification/carrier, show £220 merchant payable and **unknown delivered total**, not £264 as all-in. [Import base](https://www.gov.uk/goods-sent-from-abroad/tax-and-duty), [classification](https://www.gov.uk/trade-tariff). |
| Fees change an otherwise specified import | In the preceding conditional scenario, if a future applicable receipt establishes Royal Mail £8 handling and customs assesses D=£10, total is £220+£10+£46+£8 = **£284**. An applicable £12 Parcelforce fee instead gives **£288**. Do not add both or apply either without service evidence. [Carrier schedule](https://www.royalmail.com/receiving-mail/pay-a-fee). |
| NI low-value exception | Assume outside-UK/EU→NI, private recipient, two items and intrinsic £100. If carrier establishes at-risk treatment, the checked guidance specifies 2×€3 = **€6 duty**; at an explicitly hypothetical £0.85/€ that is £5.10. VAT, shipping, fee and applicable assessment/conversion still need evidence; **no complete total** follows. If not-at-risk is established, the low-value duty branch differs. Never transfer GB's zero-duty result automatically. [NI consumer guidance](https://www.gov.uk/goods-sent-from-abroad/tax-and-duty). |
| FX/payment scenario separate from tax FX | Assume UK-dispatched gross checkout €120 including delivery/tax, no additional import liability, charge in EUR. Hypothetical issuer rate £0.85/€ and separate 3% fee gives £102+£3.06 = **£105.06**. A hypothetical converter quote of £104 already including spread gives **£104**, without another 3% unless independently applicable. Both are conditional until the selected payment quote/fee evidence exists. Customs conversion, if needed in another route, is separately established. [Payment conversion](https://www.paypal.com/uk/webapps/mpp/ua/useragreement-full), [customs conversion](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/customs-valuation/exchange-rates). |
| Missing shipping or currency | An observed selected item £48.52 with no shipping/tax evidence is an item observation, **delivered unknown**. An amount `48.52` with no reliable currency has no valid GBP total. Do not calculate £48.52+£0+£0 or infer UK dispatch from GBP. |

## Source inspection: defaults and filters are acceptance blockers

Read-only inspection of the dirty worktree on the research date; no connector execution or fixes:

- [eBay parser](../../../src/pricerecon/connectors/ebay.py), `_parse_listings`, uses absent-price `0` and absent-currency `GBP`, leaving shipping/landed total absent. `search` sends an item `price` filter from `price_max`; that is not a delivered-cost calculation.
- [AliExpress adapter](../../../src/pricerecon/connectors/aliexpress.py), affiliate normalization, can fall back to configured currency and `display_price or Decimal("0")`; total logic can fall back to display price when shipping is missing. Some truthiness checks also fail to distinguish known free shipping from missing shipping. Requested target currency is context, not proof of response denomination.
- [Staged AliExpress browser parser](../../../src/pricerecon/connectors/aliexpress_browser.py) preserves unknown currency for a bare dollar symbol and recognizes an explicit free-shipping string. Those are useful distinctions but do not bind a generic claim to the exact destination/SKU or settle tax.
- The [audit](audit.md) also records Shopify zero/GBP defaults and post-normalization numeric item-price filtering without conversion. These retained findings need implementation-specific verification before reuse; this research does not fix them.

**Proposed gate:** missing price/currency must remain absent/quarantined with a reason; a synthetically defaulted GBP/zero cannot enter savings, ordering, baselines or alerts. Preserve provider literal and request context separately. Recognize an explicit zero only with semantic evidence (e.g. free postage), not a missing-price bargain. Recheck normalization after corrections.

Item-price filters are discovery prefilters only. “Under £150 delivered” requires qualified same-currency scenario totals after quantity, shipping, tax, fees and eligibility. Raw `price_max=150` across USD/EUR/GBP is invalid comparison. Do not discard an otherwise relevant candidate solely on a pre-discount/from-price mismatch; disclose filtering and coverage limits. Unknown-cost candidates may appear in an uncertain discovery view but cannot trigger a verified below-budget-delivered alert or win a best-comparable-total ranking.

Research supplies a proposed contract and hypothetical exercises for the ticket's planning gate. **No actual offer passes it in this asset.** Before a verified-total claim, parent review needs exact variation/condition/purchase mode, quantity, real destination/service quote, physical dispatch and consignment grouping, tax-inclusion/collector evidence, applicable classification/origin or complete import-cost coverage, carrier/other fees, selected payment scenario and freshness/revalidation evidence. AliExpress-specific UK collection behavior remains unverified beyond general terms and HMRC obligations. NI risk/assessment is a separate evidence requirement, not an inferred default.

Unresolved implementation acceptance: reject numeric/currency defaults; preserve source cost evidence; reconcile included versus added charges; exercise boundary/quantity/unknown/zero/FX cases against actual behavior. These are future verification requirements, not permission to implement, operate sources or purchase. This asset neither closes the ticket nor accepts the broader workflow, history, coupon or production gates.
