## Source coverage and reliability

The [entry-point registry](../../../pyproject.toml) includes eBay, AliExpress, Shopify, CeX, MusicMagpie, BackMarket, Vinted, Gumtree, Facebook Marketplace, numerous UK PC retailers, Dell UK, Google Shopping, Reddit communities, HotUKDeals and CamelCamelCamel. Registration is **S inventory**, not supported coverage certification. No intended watch merchant roster, brand-direct coverage matrix or UK-shipping international roster is established.

- **AliExpress — S:** [connector](../../../src/pricerecon/connectors/aliexpress.py) includes affiliate search/detail, Brave/SearXNG discovery, manual PID, DS and browser paths and coupon metadata. [Contract](../../ALIEXPRESS_CONTRACT.md) is **D**, not a receipt of today's successful workflow. `_annotate_query_match` sets `exact_variant_confirmed` and HIGH confidence from strong title-token inclusion. `_extract_ds_detail` selects `first_sku`; PID alone does not identify a chosen size/colour/strap configuration. `_extract_coupon_layers` copies metadata; browser coupon extraction captures nearby text. No reproduced checkout/stack validation follows from those fields. Some total calculations fall back to displayed price when shipping is absent. Require route-specific credential/access, selected-SKU and cost qualification.
- **eBay — S:** [search/parser](../../../src/pricerecon/connectors/ebay.py), `search`, `_parse_listings`, `_parse_browser_listings`: OAuth Browse lane or selected browser; API request capped at 50, no paging in this search path. Parser defaults missing price to zero/currency to GBP, leaves condition/shipping/total unknown, and does not preserve `buyingOptions` as an auction/fixed-price contract. Do not assert that every result is an auction; rather, buy-now status and delivered total are not proven by this normalisation. OAuth code and [eBay guide](../../EBAY_CONNECTOR.md) do not prove current account access.
- **Shopify — S:** [connector](../../../src/pricerecon/connectors/shopify.py), `_products_to_listings`: confirmed `Decimal(str(variant.get("price") or "0"))`, currency fallback GBP. Search visits a query page then reads `/products.json?limit=250`; no pagination/query filtering is performed by this method. Returns per-variant IDs but links to parent product without selected variant parameter. Browser route requires intercepted product JSON. The [fixture test](../../../tests/test_connectors.py) uses `httpx.MockTransport`; it cannot qualify real watch storefronts, currency context, complete inventory or UK delivery. These defaults can create false bargains and are foundational weaknesses, not fixes made here.
- **Second-hand — S/D:** CeX has price/condition parsing in [source](../../../src/pricerecon/connectors/cex.py); other registered sellers require their own watch relevance and access evaluation. [Vinted/Gumtree audit](../../vinted_gumtree_audit.md) calls integration production-ready but also records zero live results and deferred selector verification. Treat its readiness language as **D**, not accepted functioning coverage.
- **Discovery sources — S:** [Google Shopping](../../../src/pricerecon/connectors/google_shopping.py) declares MARKETPLACE, normalises GBP and lacks shipping totals; [Reddit/HUKD](../../../src/pricerecon/connectors/reddit.py) uses different roles for communities. Preserve discovery origin and verify merchant separately; existing SourceType alone is insufficient to prove offer authority. Search snippets/community prices cannot enter merchant baselines automatically.
- **UK PC / brand-direct — S/D:** AO and Box templates are disabled in source. [Connector status](../../CONNECTOR_STATUS.md) has historical disabled/blocked/enabled statements; [July baseline](../../connector-baseline-2026-07.md) records access failures; [Scan recovery](../../scan-recovery-2026-07.md) records 26 parsed results on 2026-07-28. These are dated **D receipts**, not current L verification or watch coverage. Dell UK source contains bot-block handling, not evidence of successful brand-direct offers.
