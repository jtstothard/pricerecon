## Answer and evidence scope

**Reuse remains a defensible design proposal, provided category policy qualifies structured offers rather than merely adding search aliases.** The counterexamples below do not require a separate PC acquisition, evidence, costing or baseline architecture. They do require explicit package/composition and compatibility semantics at the shared boundary. This research does not prove that the current implementation supports those contracts, that PC workflows work, or that generalisation is ready.

Labels: **Observed** means content returned by the web research tool in this pass, not an exercised PriceRecon connector or verified physical product. **Documented** means a first-party specification/policy or a local accepted decision; seller condition claims remain seller claims. **Inferred** marks the proposed design consequence. **Unverified** marks absent operational/offer/history evidence. Search-index excerpts are documentation leads, not live selected-offer receipts. External sources were consulted on the research date; prices displayed in documentation were not adopted as current UK offers.

## Primary-source counterexamples

| Case and primary evidence | Consequence for identity/comparison; evidence limit |
| --- | --- |
| **Near-name GPU and capacity. Documented:** NVIDIA lists RTX 4060 Ti with either 8GB or 16GB, while RTX 4060 has 8GB and different core specifications. [NVIDIA specifications](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/geforce/graphics-cards/40-series/rtx-4060-4060ti/). | **Inferred:** exact generation, base/Ti/Super suffix where applicable, desktop/laptop class, memory capacity/type and board model must survive normalization. Neither “4060”, “Ti” nor “16GB” alone is identity. A family match cannot bind the cheapest capacity. This is product-definition evidence, not merchant selection/stock. |
| **Same GPU, different board. Documented:** MSI's VENTUS 2X BLACK 16G OC specifies its own clocks, dimensions, power connector and 16GB memory. [MSI exact-board specification](https://www.msi.com/Graphics-Card/GeForce-RTX-4060-Ti-VENTUS-2X-BLACK-16G-OC/Specification). | **Inferred:** board maker/MPN, OC/revision, cooling/size and relevant included items matter. “Any RTX 4060 Ti 16GB” may be a buyer-approved alternative set; it must not silently become one exact-board history. Compatibility and recommendation are separate from exact identity. |
| **Kits versus equal aggregate capacity. Documented:** Corsair validates rated performance using the modules within one kit and warns against combining kits, even at the same rated speed. [Corsair kit guidance](https://www.corsair.com/us/en/explorer/diy-builder/memory/can-i-mix-corsair-memory-kits/). | **Inferred:** 2×16GB factory kit, one 32GB module, two separately sourced 16GB modules and one module removed from a kit are different commitments. Preserve kit MPN, module count, per-module and aggregate capacity, DDR generation/form factor, speed/timings, voltage and profiles; unknown kit provenance remains unknown. £/GB can be supplementary, never a substitute for full kit cost/equivalence. |
| **Capacity and attached accessory. Documented:** Samsung's 990 PRO announcement distinguishes standard/heatsink versions and 1/2/4TB capacities, with capacity-dependent endurance. [Samsung specifications](https://semiconductor.samsung.com/news-events/news/samsung-electronics-unveils-high-performance-990-pro-ssd-optimized-for-gaming-and-creative-applications/). | **Inferred:** exact capacity, generation/interface/form factor and factory-heatsink configuration split configurations. A separately sold heatsink or empty enclosure is not the drive. Used-drive health, write history and remaining warranty cannot be inferred from catalogue endurance or stock photography. |
| **Complete system versus named internal part. Documented:** HP describes A40Q1ET as a complete Z2 Mini G1a with Ryzen AI Max+ PRO 395, onboard 128GB memory and 1TB SSD. [HP exact-system specifications](https://www.hp.com/emea_africa-en/products/workstations/product-details/product-specifications/2103144154). | **Inferred:** a search for that processor/128GB may yield a system, board assembly or component. Preserve complete-system model/configuration, CPU, RAM, storage, OS, included peripherals and regional offering; onboard memory is not a loose DIMM offer. The regional specification supplies a counterexample, not UK availability or UK configuration equivalence. “Strix Halo” and processor synonyms remain discovery aliases, not proof that all matching systems are equivalent. |
| **Same processor, different package/support. Documented:** Intel distinguishes boxed and tray packaging and support, despite no functional difference for the same processor number; thermal-solution inclusion has exceptions. [Intel boxed/tray comparison](https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/support/articles/000029613/processors.html), [warranty policy](https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/support/articles/000024255/processors.html). | **Inferred:** package, included cooler, seller and warranty issuer/eligibility belong to the offer commitment. Do not assume a new tray CPU is used, a boxed CPU always has a cooler, or all boxed parts have identical warranty. A buyer can accept an explicit alternative, but package differences cannot disappear from exact comparable history. |
| **Condition and short promotions. Observed/documented:** Scan's Today Only page describes limited-duration/stock offers and distinguishes Open Box, Refurbished and B-Grade, including condition/accessory differences. [Scan Today Only](https://www.scan.co.uk/todayonly). | **Inferred:** retain the merchant's literal grade and its dated definition alongside normalized condition, item-specific exceptions, accessories, warranty and return terms. Do not globally equate merchant grades. Advertised promotion duration is not observed continuous availability, and basket presence does not prove reserved stock. No actual used/refurbished item's condition was verified. |
| **Generation and price regimes. Documented:** NVIDIA's 2023 announcement gives different US launch prices for RTX 4060 Ti 8/16GB; its 2025 announcement gives different launch prices for the successor RTX 5060 Ti capacities. [2023 release](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/geforce-rtx-4060-family-is-here-nvidias-revolutionary-ada-lovelace-architecture-comes-to-core-gamers-everywhere-starting-at-299), [2025 release](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2025/NVIDIA-Blackwell-GeForce-RTX-Arrives-for-Every-Gamer-Starting-at-299/default.aspx). | **Inferred:** generation, capacity and launch date cannot be collapsed into one historical series or a static “normal price.” Launch MSRP is not usual UK delivered price. **Unverified:** magnitude/direction of current UK volatility, clearance duration, price recurrence and any cross-generation value claim. No measured price trend is claimed from these releases. |

These cases challenge both false merges and false splits. Boxed/tray may share a silicon identity while differing as offers; a GPU family may intentionally contain alternatives while its boards remain distinct configurations. Shared machinery must retain both relationships without treating buyer acceptance of alternatives as evidence of identical goods.

## Reuse and category ownership

**Reuse:** existing connector/browser adapters and scheduler as acquisition substrate; shared acquisition outcomes/coverage, field-level evidence and correction lineage, selected-offer binding, observation persistence, cost/discount scenarios, baseline sufficiency/explanations, alert policy and native operational accounting. This follows the accepted [history](tickets/observation-history.md) and [operating](tickets/operating-envelope.md) decisions. Their source-audit gaps remain open, including history writing, shared cap enforcement, partial-source preservation and numeric/currency defaults; this asset supplies no implementation acceptance.

**PC policy:** required fields by subtype; generation/suffix/MPN aliases with issuer/region/revision; component/system/accessory/bundle classification; kit composition; compatibility requirements; condition and warranty descriptors; and which explicit alternative sets are acceptable for discovery. CPU socket/board firmware, RAM generation/form factor/profile, GPU power/clearance and SSD interface/form factor are suitability questions. Unknown buyer build information yields compatibility unknown, not a universal “fits.” Full automated build validation is not a prerequisite for exact-item price tracking unless the product promises suitability.

**Shared costing stays arithmetic over evidenced commitments.** Carry package count, basket contents, destination, dispatch, stock/purchase mode, eligibility and full payable components through the existing UK-cost contract. A CPU+motherboard bundle must retain the whole commitment; no invented standalone component price by subtracting a guessed motherboard value. Required add-ons/filler items remain payable basket costs. Used/new, fixed-price/auction, personal/public and uncertain cashback remain separate scenarios. Reuse current legal-cost decisions; reverify applicable rules/classification at later execution rather than assert all PC imports share one treatment.

**Shared baselines stay exact-cohort and evidence-gated.** Do not pool new with used/refurbished, factory kits with loose modules, desktop with laptop GPUs, component with system, or successor with predecessor. Broad preference discovery can rank multiple exact products side by side; it cannot present their pooled median as each product's usual price. Generation launches, stock exits, promotions, FX, source coverage and cohort corrections are separate change explanations. Use the accepted baseline-under-review/withheld output; evaluate segmentation/reset and requalification with real longitudinal data before choosing thresholds. Scarcity can increase prices; clearance can lower them. Neither direction is assumed. Seller asking price or ended listing is not a completed sale.

## Small shared-contract clarifications needed before implementation

These are proposed revisions/clarifications to the accepted conceptual contracts, not a competing schema or infrastructure build:

1. **Composition distinct from quantity:** identify the sold object/package, member roles and counts, aggregate versus per-member capacity, matched-kit status and included/excluded accessories. Quantity “1” can mean one two-module kit or one complete PC. Record unknown composition rather than flattening it into a scalar.
2. **Exact identity distinct from approved alternatives and compatibility:** give each comparison an explicit relation/policy version: exact configuration or a named, buyer-approved alternative set. Keep constituent exact histories and exclusions inspectable. Compatibility has its own evidence/context/result and cannot certify identity or price. Unknown equivalence never grants a merge.
3. **Offer commitment distinct from catalogue definition:** condition instance, package, warranty issuer/eligibility, included parts, fulfilment and purchase mode survive acquisition and corrections. Preserve merchant-local grade vocabulary and dated mapping; do not invent a globally ordered grade scale.
4. **Market regime and continuity evidence:** annotate generation/revision events and proposed regime boundaries with provenance. Shared baseline evaluation must disclose coverage changes and require review/requalification after a material discontinuity; a new generation does not automatically reset the old generation's history or import its prices.

The accepted identity/quantity/cohort contracts already accommodate much of this semantically. Parent should make these obligations explicit where downstream implementations might otherwise flatten them. No separate PC evidence store, scheduler, cost engine or forked baseline service is justified by this research. If later fixtures need category-specific arithmetic/provenance forks rather than category data/predicates, stop and revisit the shared boundary before claiming generalisation.

## Proposed future PC workflow validation protocol

Reuse the watch protocol's actual two stages: **Protocol accepted** means buyer acceptance of the cases, expected outcomes and evidence gates; **Workflow proven** requires implemented deterministic verification and separately authorised live proof. This document proposes a protocol for parent/buyer review; it does not assert either stage accepted for PC. Every gate must report **passed, failed, blocked or unproven**, evidence pointer and claim scope. Do not rename blocked access as an empty market or use a mock pass as live proof.

Future path: discover outside saved rules → retain origins/coverage → resolve exact object, package and selected offer or abstain → qualify comparable available offers → retain repeated observations/gaps → establish or withhold each baseline claim → verify coupon if available → compute exact UK scenario → show explanation → buyer corrects/preferences/tracks → verify alert behaviour.

| Gate / corpus case | Required outcome and future proof |
| --- | --- |
| Existing monitoring and discovery | Preserve existing tracked-search semantics, saved rules, enabled state, schedules, targets and last-good evidence across restart, overlap, category addition and partial-source failure. Discover a PC candidate outside saved queries and track its exact configuration without duplicate notifications. |
| GPU near-name/capacity/board traps | RTX 4060 versus Ti; Ti 8 versus 16GB; different board MPN/OC/cooling; desktop versus laptop/complete-system lead. Fixtures demonstrate rejection/abstention and distinct exact histories. Later live evidence binds actual merchant-selected SKU, price and availability; manufacturer specs alone cannot pass it. |
| RAM and bundle composition | Factory 2×16GB kit versus 1×32GB, loose module and incomplete kit; CPU+board bundle versus CPU alone. Preserve counts, full commitment and kit provenance; unknown composition withholds exact comparison. Do not divide a kit into an invented available per-module offer. |
| Systems, components and configuration | HP system counterexample, barebones/incomplete offering, SSD capacity/heatsink alternatives and accessory-only lead. Full system configuration remains distinct from CPU or board; title/URL names cannot override selection evidence. Compatibility unknown remains visible. |
| Condition and purchase mode | New boxed/tray, merchant refurbished/open-box/B-grade, unique used part, parts-only and auction. Record item-specific accessories, warranty and condition claims; separate cohorts and current bids. Images do not prove hidden damage, drive health, genuine capacity or authenticity. |
| UK total, discount and no-coupon | Exact package/quantity/whole basket/destination, shipping, tax/import/payment context and eligibility. Exercise included VAT, unknown charges, thresholds, incompatible bundles/codes and personal benefits. Unknown never becomes zero. Live coupon success needs reproduced application; no-coupon may pass independently while coupon-success remains unproven. |
| Repeated history and regime change | Qualifying repeated same-cohort observations including unchanged captures, missing periods, stock exits and real promotions. Separate genuine repricing from changed generation/capacity, seller coverage and FX. Synthetic launch/clearance fixtures prove logic only; real retained history and calibrated sufficiency are mandatory for usual-price/savings claims. No baseline is valid when evidence is insufficient. |
| Source and failure coverage | Declare PC-relevant AliExpress, eBay/second-hand, manufacturer/direct, UK retail, international UK-shipping and aggregator/community lead routes. Record attempted paths, paging/detail caps, failures and exclusions. Scan documentation readability does not pass its connector gate; one passing merchant cannot establish complete coverage. |
| Corrections and alerts | Correct wrong capacity/kit/system identity and invalidate affected bindings, cost, cohorts, history derivations and alerts while preserving tracking intent/lineage. Exercise shared-offer dedupe and restarts. Reuse one verified delivered-target-crossing alert, repeat suppression for same offer/price, further-drop and above-target/re-cross rules. Corrections, stale evidence and recovery alone do not trigger bargain alerts. |
| Capacity and buyer explanation | Tracking and potential-alert verification retain priority; discovery degrades first under caps. Prove shared admission, retry accounting, last-good preservation and recovery without catch-up bursts. Buyer walkthrough distinguishes exactness, alternatives, compatibility unknown, payable/conditional/effective cost, unavailable baselines, coverage/age and exclusions. Prototype approval cannot replace actual feedback. |

Deterministic receipts must include fixture origin, input/expected/observed outcome, assertions/failures and build/policy versions, including negative/recovery cases. Later live receipts must include timestamp, source/access lane, exact merchant selection and package, seller, condition/purchase mode, UK scenario, availability, cost components, coupon state, coverage/caps, evidence pointer, versions and limitations/recheck needs. Add PC composition/compatibility context without dropping any watch receipt fields. No retained fixture or manufacturer's launch price can replace live history.

Before any live execution/recurring activation, satisfy the watch protocol's mandatory gate: concrete numerical provider/request/page/detail/retry ceilings, browser concurrency/time, AI calls/tokens/spend if used, storage/log limits, finite duration, spend and operator-time ceilings; source entitlements and intended-use/retention rights; retention/deletion/backup treatment; freshness schedule; named maintenance/stop-resume owner; and demonstrated shared cap enforcement. Agree a small exact-PC roster and seller/build/condition preferences before collection. Account/login/cart/address actions need their own authority; stop before purchase. No cadence, budget, baseline window or freshness threshold is invented here.

## Parent decisions and next lens

Parent can review reuse with the four shared-contract clarifications above. Two sharp choices need explicit resolution before a PC implementation brief: (1) whether first PC discovery only compares exact configurations or also offers named buyer-approved alternative sets; (2) whether the product promises compatibility with a supplied build or only exposes specifications and compatibility unknowns. Neither requires a ticket created by this child. Numerical baseline/operating choices and actual source qualification already have retained gates; do not reopen them by guessing values.

Keep fountain pens as the next lens: model/revision, nib size and material, filling system, finish, complete pen versus replacement nib/feed/section, included converter and used/modified condition. A replacement nib unit and complete pen stress the same composition boundary; nominal nib sizes across brands must not be assumed equivalent. No pen taxonomy, manufacturer claim, access route or workflow is qualified by this PC research. Preserve the requirement for fresh pen-specific primary evidence and its own reviewable validation cases.
